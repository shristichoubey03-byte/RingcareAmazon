Privacy, Consent & Data Governance

RingCare is purpose-built for sensitive eldercare monitoring. Protecting your loved one's privacy, dignity, and autonomy is our highest priority.

1. What Data RingCare Collects

  • Safety-Critical Event Timestamps: Ring doorbell presses and motion detection timestamps needed to verify that your loved one is safe and up in the morning.
  • Visitor Snapshots: Low-resolution preview snapshots associated with doorbell rings to help identify legitimate visitors vs. unknown strangers.
  • Device Health Telemetry: Battery percentages and online/offline status to alert family caregivers if safety hardware goes offline.

2. What RingCare Never Collects

  • No Continuous Video or Audio Surveillance: We do not record or retain continuous audio/video feeds.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: All data is stored strictly in your local encrypted database instance. Never sold, never shared with third parties.
  • Strict Server-to-Server Calls: All communication with Amazon Vision and Ring servers occurs backend-to-backend with HMAC-SHA256 request authentication.

3. Full Data Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)

You can purge all stored visitor snapshots, timestamps, and check-in logs immediately. This action is instantaneous and irrecoverable.

Permanent Data Purge

Permanently delete all visitor logs, activity events, and check-in records from the local SQLite database.
